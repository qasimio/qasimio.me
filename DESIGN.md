# Design system / intent

## The brief

The site is not a resume and not a social network. It is a durable personal headquarters that makes Qasim's work, judgment, momentum, and humanity easy to understand.

## Visual language

Mineral teal is the primary brand color, anchored against deep ink in dark mode and warm bone in light mode. Lavender is used sparingly as a secondary signal. Large sans-serif display type provides weight; Newsreader supplies small amounts of editorial contrast; IBM Plex Mono carries metadata.

## Interaction language

Motion should feel like continuity, not decoration. View transitions, reveal timing, hover behavior, command palette, theme switching, and cursor treatment all exist to make navigation feel deliberate.

Reduced-motion users get a quiet version of the same information hierarchy.

## What the site refuses

No skill bars. No fake testimonials. No technology-logo wall. No follower counters. No fake terminal. No decorative 3D scene. No social feed. No copy that exists only to sound impressive.

## Content rule

Specific beats impressive. Concrete beats polished. A real sentence beats a slogan.
