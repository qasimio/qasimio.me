# Master direction

This version deliberately follows the structural discipline of Brittany Chiang's portfolio: a fixed/sticky identity rail, clear navigation, readable long-form sections, experience, project presentation, writing, social links, and a concise footer.

The timeline is intentionally stronger than a normal resume timeline. It records turning points in Qasim's building history, community work, shipping, open source, and professional work.

The site is multi-page. The home page is the overview. Work, Journal, About, and Contact have their own routes.

The visual system uses Brittany's public v4 navy/green palette as the primary dark mode palette, with a light mode derived from it rather than replacing the identity.
