# qasimio.me — visual thesis

This version is a deliberate synthesis of three useful qualities observed in three reference sites, without copying their layouts, code, or voice.

## What comes from where

**Brittany Chiang** → fast comprehension, strong project/experience evidence, clear navigation, and a useful archive mentality.

**Jhey Tompkins** → interaction as explanation, small playful demos, responsive micro-behaviors, and a sense that the browser itself can be part of the portfolio.

**Zach Holman** → owned voice, current/previous framing, chronological writing, plain language, and a site that can remain personal even as the person's career gets larger.

## What makes the combination ours

The site is a personal headquarters for a builder who moves between software, products, open source, and communities. It is not a résumé, not a social network, and not a design exercise.

The hierarchy is:

1. What Qasim builds.
2. Evidence that he actually ships.
3. How he thinks about the work.
4. Who he is outside the code.
5. What he is doing now.

Motion should reinforce navigation, state, and personality. It should never exist just to prove that CSS can move.
