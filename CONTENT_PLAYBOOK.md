# Content playbook

Write like a person who was actually there.

Prefer:

- concrete details
- contractions
- short and long sentences mixed together
- honest uncertainty
- what changed after shipping
- mistakes, surprises, constraints, tradeoffs

Avoid:

- “passionate about”
- “leveraging”
- “cutting-edge”
- “in today's rapidly evolving world”
- generic claims about innovation
- inflated adjectives
- perfect corporate transitions

For Notes, one thought is enough. Do not manufacture an essay because the page has whitespace.
