# Content rules

Write like a person who was there.

Prefer:

- concrete verbs
- specific failures
- real constraints
- actual outcomes
- short sentences mixed with longer ones
- first person when the detail is personal

Avoid:

- "passionate"
- "cutting-edge"
- "leveraging"
- "revolutionary"
- vague claims about impact
- corporate mission language
- fake certainty

For metrics, always label the unit and the source context.

For credentials, use the official name.

For projects, explain why they exist before explaining the stack.
