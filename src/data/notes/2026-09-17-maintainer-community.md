title: 'I got invited to GitHub’s Maintainer Community.'
date: 2026-09-17
excerpt: 'That one felt different. Mostly because it arrived after doing the boring part: maintaining software in public.'
tag: 'Open source'
---

I did not wake up one morning with a sudden desire to become a “maintainer.” I just kept fixing things in public and eventually GitHub noticed.

The useful part is not the invite itself. It is the reminder that open source gets interesting when there are actual people on the other side of the issue tracker.
