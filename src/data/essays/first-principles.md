There is a recurring pattern in my projects: I start with a feature and eventually end up rebuilding the part underneath it.

Search became an index. Document chat became an ingestion system. Coding assistance became filesystem verification.

I do not think this is always the right instinct. Sometimes the boring managed service is exactly what a project needs. But when I cannot explain the machinery underneath a system, I usually feel like I am borrowing confidence from a black box.

That is the thread I keep pulling.
