## The problem

University project work often disappears after the grade is recorded. The repository might exist, but people rarely see the thing itself, who built it, or what happened afterward.

Launchpad is an attempt to change that at SIBA.

## What I built around it

The community is centered on showcasing deployed or nearly-finished work, encouraging people to publish what they built, and creating room for collaboration around projects without turning the main showcase into a general chat group.

The broader ecosystem also uses GitHub data to make project and contributor information easier to discover.

## The interesting part

The hard part is not the website.

It is lowering the social cost of publishing your work when you know other students are going to look at it.
