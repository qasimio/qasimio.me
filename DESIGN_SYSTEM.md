# Design system

## Visual idea

Editorial software journal: the information density and clarity of a strong engineer portfolio, the playfulness of a front-end experimenter, and the voice of a personal publishing site.

## Palette

- Ink: `#071114`
- Bone: `#f2f0ea`
- Teal: `#28b8ad` light / `#4ed7ca` dark
- Violet: `#7057e8` light / `#a493ff` dark
- Coral: `#d86f59` light / `#f09a84` dark

Teal is used for action and product/building. Violet is used for ideas and writing. Coral is used sparingly for people/contact.

## Typography

- Manrope variable for interface and heavy display
- Source Serif 4 variable for selective editorial emphasis
- IBM Plex Mono for metadata

The main body should remain comfortable to read. No microtext below 9px in the actual interface.

## Inspiration synthesis

Brittany Chiang: clear hierarchy, direct identity, experience/project evidence, archive mentality.

Jhey Tompkins: interaction should make an idea click, demos and micro-behaviors can teach without taking over the page.

Zach Holman: current/previous framing, prolific chronological writing, personal voice, plain language, and a site that feels owned rather than branded by committee.

The result deliberately avoids copying any of their layouts or visual identities.
